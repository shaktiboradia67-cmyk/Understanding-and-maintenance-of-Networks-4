## Ethernet Cables vs Fiber-Optic Cables

| No. | Ethernet Cable | Fiber-Optic Cable |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Transmission Medium | Transmits data using electrical signals through copper wires. | Transmits data using pulses of light through optical fibers. |
| 2. Speed and Distance | Commonly used for connections up to 100 meters per Ethernet link, depending on the cable and standard. | Supports high bandwidth and much longer distances, depending on the fiber type and equipment. |
| 3. Interference | Can be affected by electromagnetic interference, depending on the cable type and environment. | Immune to electromagnetic interference. |

### Real-World Examples

1. **Ethernet Cable:** Used to connect a desktop computer or gaming PC to a home router or network switch.
2. **Fiber-Optic Cable:** Used as a backbone connection between buildings across a college campus or to connect equipment in a data center.

## Practical: Attaching an RJ45 Connector to a CAT6 Cable

### Objective
To learn how to attach an RJ45 connector to a CAT6 Ethernet cable using an RJ45 crimping tool.

### Reference Video
Watch this YouTube tutorial demonstrating how to crimp an RJ45 connector:

[How to Crimp Cat5 / Cat6 Network Patch Cables — Switched On Network](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QMpWkkqX1eM)

### Tools and Materials Required
- CAT6 Ethernet cable
- RJ45 connector compatible with CAT6 cable
- RJ45 crimping tool
- Cable stripper or wire cutter
- Ethernet cable tester

### Step-by-Step Procedure

**Step 1: Cut the Cable**  
Cut the CAT6 cable to the required length using a cable cutter.

**Step 2: Remove the Outer Jacket**  
Carefully strip approximately 2–3 cm of the outer insulation without damaging the internal wires.

**Step 3: Separate the Wires**  
Separate the four twisted pairs and straighten the eight individual wires carefully.

**Step 4: Arrange the Wires in T568B Order**  
Arrange the wires in the following sequence:

1. White-Orange
2. Orange
3. White-Green
4. Blue
5. White-Blue
6. Green
7. White-Brown
8. Brown

**Step 5: Trim the Wires**  
Cut the wires evenly so that they can fit properly inside the RJ45 connector. Keep the exposed wire length short and minimize untwisting.

**Step 6: Insert the Wires into the RJ45 Connector**  
Insert all eight wires into the connector in the correct order. Ensure that each wire reaches the front of its channel and that the outer cable jacket extends into the connector's strain-relief area.

**Step 7: Crimp the Connector**  
Place the RJ45 connector into the compatible crimping tool and squeeze the handles firmly to secure the contacts and cable.

**Step 8: Inspect and Test the Cable**  
Check that the wires are in the correct order and the connector is secure. Use an Ethernet cable tester to verify that all eight connections are correctly wired.

### Precautions
- Use an RJ45 connector designed for the specific CAT6 cable type.
- Avoid damaging the internal wires while stripping the jacket.
- Follow the T568B color sequence carefully.
- Use a cable tester before connecting the cable to network equipment.

### Result
The RJ45 connector was attached to the CAT6 Ethernet cable using the T568B wiring standard. The cable is ready for network use after a successful continuity and wiring test.

## Choosing Network Cables and Tools for a Gaming Café

I would use **Ethernet cables (CAT6)** to connect all 10 PCs because they provide fast and reliable connections for LAN gaming with low latency. Since the PCs are in the same café, the cable distances are short, so fiber optics are not necessary. I would use an **RJ45 crimper** to attach RJ45 connectors to the Ethernet cables.
